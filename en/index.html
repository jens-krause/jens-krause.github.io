01 Tools

Picks the sharpest frame out of a burst of wildlife shots so you don’t have to zoom into every one. It scores an 18×12 grid of tiles with a Laplacian filter, weights them by camera auto‑focus area, and flags the winner per stack. The tool ranks sharpness for the animal's eye, not the grass behind it.
